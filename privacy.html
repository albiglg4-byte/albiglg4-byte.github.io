Privacy Policy

What data the App uses

  • Basic TikTok profile info (open id, display name, avatar) via the user.info.basic scope – used only to show which account is connected.
  • TikTok access and refresh tokens – used only to upload videos to your own account.
  • Video files and titles created by the App on your computer.

Where it is stored

All data is stored locally on your computer. The App has no servers and does not send your data to the developer or any third party. Data goes only to TikTok, through TikTok's official API, when a video is uploaded.

Deleting data

Delete the App folder to remove all locally stored data, and revoke the App's access in your TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions).
